My Story

My name is Lethabo David Mogashoa and I am a young, ambitious South African with a deep passion for technology, communication, and community impact. Growing up, I was always curious about how things worked — whether it was a radio station, a website, or a mobile app.

That curiosity eventually led me to pursue web development through the CodeTribe Bootcamp, where I am learning to turn ideas into real digital products. Every line of code I write is a step closer to building something that matters.

Media & Broadcasting

One of my proudest experiences was visiting and participating at Energy FM, one of South Africa's beloved radio stations. Sitting behind the microphone, interacting with the audience, and understanding how media connects people deeply inspired me to combine storytelling with technology in my work.
